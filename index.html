Outcome Context

A case-study prototype for a Groww-like investing app. Scope: a first-time investor's first IPO listing outcome. Mock data throughout.

Tap through the phone, or jump here.

The feature does

  • Explains what the result is
  • Separates outcome from decision quality
  • Offers neutral context and one reflection
  • Leaves Buy and Sell untouched

The feature never

  • Recommends buy, sell or hold
  • Predicts returns or judges suitability
  • Rates the user as a good or bad investor
  • Blocks or delays an action
Illustrative mock data. Not investment advice.
